## ✨ Why HausaBench-RLHF?

Hausa is spoken by **80M+ people across West Africa**, yet frontier LLMs frequently fail on:
1. **Financial Fraud & BVN/MoMo Social Engineering in Hausa:** Models often fail to refuse smishing templates or fail to warn users against predatory digital loan apps.
2. **Pediatric & Maternal Health Triage:** Models hallucinate unsafe herbal treatments for severe pediatric dehydration instead of advising immediate ORS and clinic referral.
3. **Agrarian & Technical Code-Switching:** Farmers and STEM students require accurate Hausa terminology for nitrogen leaching, SQL injection prevention, and civic USSD workflows (`*996#`).
